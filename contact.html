Short bio

Babykelo (Aramogho Oghenekevwe Samuel) is a Nigerian drummer, creative producer, music director, live arranger and mixing engineer from Delta State. One of Nigeria's most sought-after drummers, he has worked with Wizkid, Ada Ehi, Tim Godfrey and Masterkraft, and is a Flystickz and Centent Cymbals artist.

Full bio

Aramogho Oghenekevwe Samuel, popularly known as Babykelo, was born on December 22nd and hails from Delta State, Nigeria. He is an outstanding drummer, creative producer, unique music director, excellent live arranger and mixing engineer.

His love for drumming started in childhood, when he played every object around him like a drum. His mother's love for good music, especially the sounds of the 1980s, shaped his rhythmic and melodic foundation.

A band finalist at Star Quest 2012 (3rd place), he is music director for Voice of Karis (V.O.K) and band leader of MaskTunes. He has worked with Wizkid, Ada Ehi, Dr. Eddy, Tim Godfrey, Masterkraft, Ramoni, DJ Tunez, Victony and Ccioma. He is an endorsed Flystickz artist with his own BabyKelo Signature Series 5B drumsticks, and a Centent Cymbals & Gongs artist.
