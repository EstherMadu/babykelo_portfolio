01 — Origin

The boy who drummed on everything.

Born on December 22nd and raised with the rhythms of Delta State, Aramogho Oghenekevwe Samuel was tapping out grooves on tables, buckets, doors, anything that would answer back, long before he ever owned a pair of sticks.

His mother loved good music. Through her, the sounds of the 1980s filled the house, and those records built the rhythmic and melodic foundation he still plays from. Highlife swing, gospel fire, funk pocket, the talking drum's voice: it's all in there.

Today Babykelo is one of Nigeria's most sought-after professional drummers. He's a creative producer, a music director, a live arranger and a mixing & mastering engineer, and he has shared stages with Wizkid, Ada Ehi and Tim Godfrey, among many others.
